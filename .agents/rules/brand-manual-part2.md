- **Rule**: Never mix more than one serif and one sans-serif in a single screen or document.

## 3. Verbal Guardrails (Words to Use vs. Words to Avoid)

| Use | Avoid | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Practical, illuminate, system, capability, adopt** | Empower, revolutionize, disrupt, game-changer | Avoid generic NGO/startup buzzwords; project institutional maturity |
| **Farm, agribusiness, operator, learner** | Beneficiary, stakeholder (in customer copy) | Clients are entrepreneurs and professionals, not charity cases |
| **Show, teach, build, equip** | Help (used alone without a verb) | "Help" is vague; always pair with a concrete action |

## 5. Canonical Company Story (About Page)
> Light Agribusiness Consultancy Limited began with a simple, stubborn observation: across African agriculture, the biggest constraint was rarely the land, the weather, or even the capital. It was the shortage of practical knowledge — people who knew not just the science of farming, but the business of running one.
>
> What started as agribusiness consulting grew into something larger, because the work kept revealing the same pattern: clients did not just need advice, they needed to be taught, mentored, and equipped with systems that would keep working long after the engagement ended. And increasingly, they needed technology that could turn all of that into something they could see and manage daily.
>
> Today, Light stands on three pillars that reflect that reality. The Light Agribusiness Academy trains the next generation of practitioners and professionals. Our Advisory practice builds the operational systems that turn agricultural effort into investable, scalable business. And LightAgriOS, our proprietary technology platform, gives farms, cooperatives, and enterprises the tools to manage, track, and grow with real data, not guesswork.
>
> Our name is not incidental. “Light” reflects what we believe our role in African agriculture should be: illuminating a path that is often unclear, so that students, farmers, agripreneurs, and enterprises alike can move forward with confidence rather than trial and error.

## 6. Customer Journey Maps (6-Stage Lifecycle)

### Journey 1: Chidinma (Aspiring Agripreneur, Academy Pathway)
- **Awareness**: "Is agribusiness a real career?" → IG/TikTok reels, friends' SIWES posts. Show modern profitability.
- **Consideration**: "Which training is credible?" → Course page, reviews, WhatsApp group. Real curriculum and graduate outcomes.
- **Decision**: "Can I afford this?" → Transparent pricing, flexible options, clear certificate value.
- **Onboarding**: "Will this be worth my time?" → First live session delivers immediate usable takeaway by day one.
- **Growth**: "What's next?" → Alumni network, internship/SIWES placement board, advanced certs.
- **Advocacy**: "I want my friends to know." → Graduation posts, testimonial reels.

### Journey 2: Tunde (Scaling Farm Operator, Advisory + LightAgriOS Pathway)
- **Awareness**: "Farm is growing but losing control." → Articles on record-keeping, cashflow, yield tracking.
- **Consideration**: "Will I get a generic report?" → Concrete diagnostic deliverables and case studies.
- **Decision**: "What exactly do I get?" → Scoped, fixed-fee diagnostic offer with concrete deliverables.
- **Onboarding**: "Do they understand my farm floor?" → Farm visit demonstrating hands-on competence.
- **Growth**: "How do I sustain this?" → SOP handover + LightAgriOS setup.
- **Advocacy**: "Other farmers should know." → Farmer groups, co-ops, shareable before/after data.

### Journey 3: Mrs. Adaeze (Corporate Agribusiness Executive, Enterprise Pathway)
- **Awareness**: "Need external partner with agricultural depth." → LinkedIn articles, executive conferences.
- **Consideration**: "Can they deliver at scale?" → Institutional proposals, audited case studies, reference calls.
- **Decision**: "What is the ROI?" → Board-level framing: operational efficiency, compliance, investment-readiness.
- **Onboarding**: "Will this disrupt operations?" → Structured project management with department heads.
- **Growth**: "Can this scale across other sites?" → Multi-farm LightAgriOS dashboard and benchmarking.
- **Advocacy**: "Peer companies should hear." → Joint case studies and conference keynotes.

## 7. Offer Ladders & Delivery Models

### Academy (Knowledge & Capacity)
- **Ladder**: Free webinars/guides → Entry short courses → Mid-tier certifications → Institutional SIWES/corporate training → Flagship Annual Summit.
- **Certification Tiers**:
  - *Foundation*: Single-topic short courses (completion quiz + short practical assignment).
  - *Professional*: Multi-week role-oriented (assessed project/case study + practical demo).
  - *Advanced / Executive*: Enterprise/investor-readiness (capstone evaluated by expert panel).

### Advisory & Business Systems
- **Ladder**: Entry (fixed-fee Diagnostic) → Mid-tier (SOP design, feasibility) → Premium (transformation, retainers) → Institutional (M&E).
- **5-Stage Delivery Model**: **Diagnose → Design → Document → Deploy → Sustain**.
  - *Sustain Phase*: Integrates LightAgriOS to maintain systems after the consulting team exits.

### Technology (LightAgriOS)
- **Ladder**: Free tier (basic records) → SME tier (crop/livestock, inventory, finance) → Professional tier (analytics, decision support) → Enterprise tier (multi-farm monitoring, investor dashboard) → Marketplace & Training Center.

## 8. Monetization & The High-Leverage Bundled Offer
- **The Core Conversion Move**: An Advisory diagnostic engagement bundled with **6–12 months of LightAgriOS**. Converts one-off consulting fees into high-margin recurring SaaS subscriptions.
- **Pricing Logic**:
  - *Academy*: Priced for volume, top-of-funnel reach, and trust.
  - *Advisory*: Value-based pricing tied to client scale and ROI (never billable hours).
  - *LightAgriOS*: Free/low barrier entry for network effects; high-value pricing for enterprise dashboards & multi-site tools.

## 9. Marketing, Social & Content Architecture

### Social Media Jobs-by-Platform
- **LinkedIn (3–4x/wk)**: Institutional authority, B2B/enterprise, investors, founder thought leadership.
- **Facebook (Daily)**: Broad reach to practicing farmers and older agripreneurs, practical tips, Q&A.
- **Instagram (5–7x/wk)**: Student/young professional awareness, reels, graduate spotlights.
- **X / Twitter (3–5x/wk)**: Real-time commentary, agribusiness policy, industry trends threads.
- **YouTube (1–2x/wk)**: Deep-dive education, training excerpts, farm case studies, software demos.
- **TikTok (5–7x/wk)**: Fast, myth-busting, "day in the life" youth awareness.
- **WhatsApp**: Community retention, cohort communications, real-time client support.
- **Email**: Weekly/biweekly nurture newsletters, course launches, investor updates.

### Employee Brand Handbook (Internal Culture)
1. **Teach before you tell**: Any internal communication must educate with practical clarity.
2. **Leave something written**: No verbal-only handoffs; always produce an SOP, checklist, or note.
3. **Cross-pillar awareness is mandatory**: Every team member must be able to articulate all three pillars and their cross-referrals.

### Promise-Keeping Mechanisms
- Every advisory engagement ends with a **one-page "What To Do Next" action sheet**.
- Every training session ends with a **practical assignment completed within 7 days**.
- Every LightAgriOS onboarding includes a **guided first-week setup**, not a self-serve manual dump.
- The **Clarity Checkpoint**: Every engagement ends with what is now clear, what system now exists, and what changes on Monday morning.
